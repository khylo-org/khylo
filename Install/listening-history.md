# Listening and watching history

Khylo can remember what you listen to and watch, so your own apps can show your weekly stats and a year-end review.

## What is recorded

For each song, audiobook, podcast episode, movie and show episode you play: what it was, when it started, how long you actually
listened or watched, and which app and device you used. Titles are copied at that moment, so your history stays right if you
later rename or remove something in your library. It is kept in its own file in Khylo's data folder and is included in
Khylo's backups.

Nothing from your history goes into the activity log, the server logs or any support files.

## Who can see it

- **Your listening is only ever seen by you.** Your weekly, quarterly and yearly reviews are private. Nobody else can open them,
  including the person who runs the server.
- **The person who runs the server can see what is played and watched** on the server, the way other media servers show it,
  but not your reviews.

## Turning it off and deleting it

- **Record my listening** is on by default. Turn it off and Khylo records nothing more for you; what is already stored stays
  until you delete it.
- **Delete all of my history** removes everything stored about you. Deleting a person's account deletes their history too.
