# Mirroring

Mirroring plays the same thing on more than one of your TVs (or any of your devices) at once. Start something on one device, pick the others, and they follow it: they start the same movie or episode from the same moment, pause and resume with it, and are moved back into step if they drift apart.

## How to use it

- **Android TV:** while something is playing, open the player's menu and choose **Mirror**. Pick the TVs that should follow this one. Pick a TV again to stop it following.
- **Web:** click the handoff icon in the top bar while something is playing, then pick the screens under **Mirror what I'm watching on**.

Every device you pick has to be open and signed in as you. A device that isn't shows up as not found.

## What to expect

- The TVs keep themselves within about a fifth of a second of each other, and always inside one second, once they have settled. A TV that falls behind plays very slightly faster until it catches up (you will not notice), and one that is far out jumps. It is not frame-perfect: in the same room you may hear a faint echo, so mute all but one.
- A TV needs a few seconds after it starts to settle, because it has to load the stream first.
- Starting a new movie or episode on the device you are following starts it on the others too. When you stop watching, the others stop a few seconds later.
- If someone stops a TV that is following, or starts something else on it, that TV leaves the mirror and carries on by itself.
- Live TV and Khylo TV channels can be mirrored: the other TVs tune to the same channel and follow when it changes. A channel runs on the clock, so they land on the same moment. Khylo TV channels are followed by Android TV for now.
- Your Khylo Server must be up to date. An older server doesn't have Mirror, and the app says so.
- Mirroring only ever links your own devices. It never moves anything between different people.
- Mirrors end when the server restarts. Start them again from the player.
